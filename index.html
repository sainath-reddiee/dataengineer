About the author

Articles on this site are written and maintained by Sainath Reddy, a practicing data engineer who builds production data platforms on Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Azure, GCP, Salesforce Data Cloud, dbt, Apache Airflow and Apache Spark. Every tutorial comes from real engineering work, not reposted material, and is checked before publication for technical accuracy and current vendor behaviour.

Coverage spans cloud data warehousing (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift), lakehouse architectures (Databricks, Delta Lake, Iceberg), orchestration (Airflow, Snowflake Tasks, Step Functions), transformation (dbt, SQL, PySpark), streaming (Kafka, Kinesis, Snowpipe Streaming), AI on data (Snowflake Cortex, Databricks Mosaic AI, RAG patterns), data quality and governance, FinOps, and certification guidance.
